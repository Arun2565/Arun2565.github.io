## How I make these posts

This is a daily log of what I read. Here is how I put it together.

### Finding articles

I spend time each day reading blogs, newsletters, and research posts. I find them through Twitter, Reddit, Hacker News, and email newsletters. I also get recommendations from friends and from other writers I follow.

I do not read everything. I scan headlines and pick the ones that look useful or interesting. If I can summarize the main idea in one sentence, I read the full article.

### Reading and taking notes

I read on my phone, my laptop, or in an EPUB reader. Sometimes I convert articles to EPUB so I can read them offline with better formatting. I built a small tool for this that pulls the article text, strips out the ads and navigation, and saves it as an EPUB file.

While I read, I highlight the parts that matter. I look for:
- The main argument or claim
- Any data or examples that support it
- Rules or principles I want to remember
- Things I disagree with or want to think more about

I do not take detailed notes. I just mark the parts that stick.

### Writing the post

At the end of the day, I pick 3 to 5 articles that were worth my time. For each one, I write a short summary in my own words. I try to explain what the article says and why it matters, without copying the original text.

I link to every article so you can read the full version if you want. I am not reviewing or critiquing these pieces. I am just sharing what I found useful.

### Publishing

I write the post in markdown and push it to my blog. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It takes about a minute to go live after I push.

I try to post every day, but I miss days when I am busy or when nothing I read feels worth sharing. That is fine. The goal is consistency, not perfection.

### Tools I use

- **Reading:** Browser, EPUB reader, Python scripts for converting articles
- **Writing:** Markdown editor, GitHub
- **Publishing:** GitHub Pages, Jekyll

That is it. Simple process, no fancy tools, just daily reading and a short write-up.
